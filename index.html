Ways to host

Specialisation is the product. The OpenRouter catalogue is what we are preparing now. The other three are open for inquiry, and none of them is sold yet.

  • 01 · In preparation

    OpenRouter

    Coding profiles for C++, Python, and other languages, on one open-weight Qwen3.8 model. A profile is a style of that model, not a separate set of weights.

    Ask about OpenRouter

  • 02 · Waiting list

    Flat-rate lanes

    A specialised model booked by concurrency and context window. Inside that envelope there is no per-token meter. The envelope is the limit.

    Join the waiting list

  • 03 · Waiting list

    Dedicated GPUs

    A private-cloud server or a booked slice of specific GPUs for your models, in Europe. Not a shared pool.

    Ask about dedicated GPUs

  • 04 · Waiting list

    On premises

    The same dedicated shape, installed at your site, as a B2B project with hardware.

    Ask about on premises

Cloud hosting is in Europe. On-premises projects run on hardware at your site. Prompts and completions are not used to train models and are not handed to third-party inference providers.

What a lane will be specified by

Nothing on this page is a benchmark result. When numbers appear here, each one will come from a recorded measurement run on the exact hardware and configuration offered.

  • Output tokens per second and prompt tokens per second, reported separately
  • Time to first token and time per output token, including per-user lower percentiles
  • Aggregate throughput at stated concurrency, not a single-stream headline
  • Board power and whole-server wall power, with joules per output token
  • Queue behaviour and overflow handling once a lane is saturated
  • Context length and KV-cache capacity for the exact served build

Figures are published only after the measurement passes review. Until then this section stays descriptive — deliberately.
