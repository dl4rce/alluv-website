04 / SPECIFICATION

Measured,
not implied.

Every commercial lane will be described by its actual hardware, model build, context window and concurrency envelope. Numbers will be published only after a recorded measurement passes review.

  • Output and prompt tokens per second, reported separately
  • Time to first token and time per output token
  • Aggregate throughput at stated concurrency
  • Board and whole-server power, with energy per output token
  • Queue behavior and overflow handling at saturation
  • Context length and KV-cache capacity for the served build
